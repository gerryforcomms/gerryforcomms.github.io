Working Class Cat is a selection of hand-drawn comic strips and caricatures. 

These works are inspired by the original character, the working class cat, and photographs of [Pontus Pilates Kjellgren](https://gerry.website/posts/a-study-of-pontus/), a cat with quite the reputation in the Kjellgren family’s social circle. 

The drawings explore stigma, thoughts, struggles and joy that work brings, through 2 key feline characters - a sooty, grumpy-looking working class cat, and the very well-loved, legendary Pontus, whose stories are still being told by humans who have had the privilege of meeting him.

![An opened page of the zine, Working Class Cat, showing a comic strip about entrepreneurship.](/WorkingClassCat_open.jpg)

The small-print zine premieres at [Kulturhuset Leoparden](https://www.kulturhusetleoparden.com/)'s art market on 9 May 2026 in Uppsala, Sweden, with 50 serialized copies available, alongside prints and stickers.
